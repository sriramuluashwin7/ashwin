Long-term interest: Koopman operators and digital twins

Koopman operator theory and Hilbert-space methods, and how they relate to the latent structure that neural networks learn. I have outlined a framework that links regime-invariant feature learning with building digital twins from first principles, drawing on Koopman operators, Volterra series and invariant causal prediction.

Own switched reluctance motor Longer term A 12/10 outer-rotor design, validated by finite-element simulation, meant to drive the rovers.
  • DC-excited, doubly salient, outer rotor; 12 stator poles and 10 rotor poles.
  • Validated by finite-element simulation in FEMM.

  • Meant to drive Arvorean’s rovers, and may also be offered to other builders of mobile robots. A conference paper is planned.
