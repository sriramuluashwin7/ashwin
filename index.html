Arvorean Robotics and Automation

Founder and Director since 2026. A private limited company registered in Tamil Nadu, corporate identification number U62010TN2026PTC195993.

Automation against exploitation.

The company exists to keep people away from backbreaking and dangerous manual labour, such as moving half a tonne of goods an hour by hand in a warehouse or factory. Warehouses and factories come first; mines and similar places may follow.

  • First product

    The rover

    A wheeled rover built from bought and fabricated parts, so that the main risk lies in integration rather than in new invention. The current build is designed for a 30 kg payload, with an aluminium-extrusion chassis, four planetary gearmotors each with its own driver, a Raspberry Pi 4B for vision and a Raspberry Pi Pico 2 for motor control; the vision pipeline is under development. A separate four-motor tank-drive version, controlled from a web page through an ESP32, is already built. The firmware is designed as a strictly layered stack around a 1 kHz deterministic control loop, with a bridge to ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2). The plan is to rent rovers out as a fleet and to sell the same hardware as a research platform.

  • Next

    Laboratory kits

    A motor-control laboratory kit for university courses that follow the Anna University R2025 and SRM R2021 curricula. It is designed around a custom circular circuit board with an RP2350A microcontroller, a BTS7960B H-bridge driver and five motor modules, with motor speed estimated from an ADXL345 accelerometer by fast Fourier transform. An analogue characterisation kit and a 555-timer experimenter kit are planned to follow.

  • Longer term

    A switched reluctance motor

    A motor of my own design: a DC-excited, doubly-salient, outer-rotor machine with 12 stator poles and 10 rotor poles, validated by finite-element simulation in FEMM. It is to be paired with its own controller and a fixed, calculated gear reduction, so that the motor runs near its most efficient speed. Once mature it is meant to drive Arvorean’s rovers, and it may also be offered to other builders of mobile robots as a propulsion module. The design is the subject of a planned conference paper; the drawing at the top of this page shows its topology in schematic form.

Research

My long-term interest is Koopman operator theory and Hilbert-space methods, and how they relate to the latent structure that neural networks learn. I have outlined a framework that connects regime-invariant feature learning with the construction of digital twins from first principles, drawing on Koopman operators, Volterra series and invariant causal prediction. My applied work, listed below, covers engine emissions, battery degradation, embedded control, classifier generalisation and speech recognition.

Abstract accepted

Hybrid digital twin of lithium-ion battery packs

Accepted as an abstract at SAE WCX (SAE International’s World Congress Experience); work in progress. The paper proposes a digital twin that couples physics simulation in MATLAB Simscape Battery Builder with recurrent neural networks (long short-term memory and gated recurrent unit networks, LSTM and GRU) to estimate capacity fade, resistance growth, state of health and remaining useful life, and that uses observed patterns of degradation to suggest second-life uses for retired electric-vehicle (EV) packs. The simulated dataset is planned for open release.

In preparation

ThermoStateNet

A GRU-based framework for predicting nitrogen oxide (NOx) emissions from engines, in which the latent space is linearised along the lines of Koopman operator theory. It is evaluated on two engine datasets, with NASA’s C-MAPSS turbofan data as a cross-domain test, and its PyTorch code base is complete.

Decision boundary fractal complexity

A measure proposed as a classifier-agnostic predictor of the generalisation gap on tabular data: the box-counting dimension of the 1-nearest-neighbour decision boundary, measured after projecting the features with principal component analysis. A complete draft exists.

ProsGuide

A parameter-efficient adapter for speech recognition that feeds four simple prosodic features (fundamental frequency, energy, the change in energy, and zero-crossing rate) into a frozen Whisper-small encoder through cross-attention. First experiments on LibriSpeech are complete and a manuscript draft exists; a study of where in the encoder the adapter is best placed is planned.

Current

Decentralised coordination of EV charging

With a small team I am working towards a method for coordinating electric-vehicle charging without a central server, using pseudonymous messages exchanged between vehicles. The single objective is that no station is overcrowded while others stand empty. The reason for leaving out the server is privacy: a central server would let one company follow individual drivers’ routes over time. Early stage.
